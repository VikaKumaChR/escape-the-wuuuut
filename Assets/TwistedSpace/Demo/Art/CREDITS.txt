Displaced Order / original demo art
==================================

These PNG textures and geometric icons were procedurally authored for this project.
No third-party texture pack, copied game UI, paid asset or external image-generation
service was used.

PanelGlass: nine-slice dark instrument glass with a restrained cyan edge.
ButtonFace / ButtonHalo: nine-slice button field and soft hover outline.
IconObserve / IconReset / IconComplete / IconDoor / IconPlay / IconHint:
transparent geometric glyphs, tinted in Unity rather than duplicated by state.
Concrete / BrushedMetal / FloorPanels: small tileable 3D surface textures.
OpeningThreshold: original architectural key art for the fixed opening menu.
The lettering and button remain editable UGUI, not text baked into the image.

All lighting and glass effects remain local to the material/UI. These assets do not
require camera blur, bloom, screen-space distortion or a third-party animation package.
